## RNA and DNA Re-extractions for ENCORE Project, June 6, 2025

### [Protocol Link](https://github.com/nchampney/NEC_Putnam_Lab_Notebook/blob/172f01784174bebceccae246c713908b109356b2/protocols/2025-05-28-Protocols_Zymo_Quick_DNA_RNA_Miniprep_Plus.md)

### Re-extraction of 8 samples from ENCORE project that did not yield adequate results the first time. The samples were collected in July and August 2024. 

### Samples

These samples were selected chosen to be re-extracted after having low yields the first time. The ID numbers are MD-2-25, MD-2-17, MD-4-12, MD-5-14, MD-1-8, MD-1-22, MD-4-5, and MD5-24.

## Notes

- Followed the linked protocol exactly 
- For sample prep bead beat was used in the original tube and vortexed for and additional 1 minute. 
- Eluted to 80 uL 
    - for samples MD-2-25, MD-2-17, MD-4-12, and MD-5-14 the DNA was extracted to 100 uL

## Qubit Results
